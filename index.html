About the show

Thom Pain (based on nothing) is Will Eno's one-man play, a finalist for the 2005 Pulitzer Prize for Drama. This Gold Coast production stars Ty Jordan, directed by Jake Goodall, at Javeenbah Theatre in Nerang as part of the One Person One Acts season.

One man alone on stage, talking straight to the audience. He keeps coming back to a few stories: a boyhood dog, a bee sting, a woman he loved. He interrupts himself, picks on the room, and never quite gets to his point.

Looking for something to see on the Gold Coast this weekend? The final show is Saturday 24 October at 7:30pm.
